Radicals: 
Radicals  are  the  essential  components  of  Chinese  characters.  For 
example, in the Chinese character 你 (meaning “you”), the left part 
亻is  a  radical  that  evolved  from  the  earliest  visual  representation 
of  a  person.  Radicals  are  often  used  to  illustrate  meanings.  In  the 
example  of  the  character  你  ,  the  radical  亻indicates  “person”.
