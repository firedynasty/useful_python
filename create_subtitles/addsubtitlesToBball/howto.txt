How to create subtitled highlight clips
=======================================

1. Download the video (if you only have the .m4a):

   yt-dlp -f "bestvideo[ext=mp4]+bestaudio[ext=m4a]" "https://youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID" --merge-output-format mp4

2. Edit add_titles.md with your notes (one per line):

   M:SS, your comment
   e.g.
   1:17, need to pass at an angle
   1:39, here can get the slip angle

3. Run the script:

   bash add_titles.sh "Understanding Princeton Offense： Systems, Entries & Variations [uzZKtYbhHq0].mp4"

4. Output: *_subtitled.mp4 (a highlights reel of just the noted moments with captions)

Notes
-----
- Each clip shows 3 seconds before the note, then holds the caption for 10 seconds
- The caption displays the original timestamp and your comment (e.g. "1:17, need to pass at an angle")
- Requires: ffmpeg, python3
