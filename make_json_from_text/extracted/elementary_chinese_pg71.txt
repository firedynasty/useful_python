Grammar Notes: 
1.  多 (duō, how, to what extent): 
It is an adverb, often used before an adjective to ask about the 
degree or extent of something. The structure is “Subj. + 多多 + 
Adj. ?” , meaning “”How [adjective] is [subject]?” In this 
dialogue, the question phrase 多大 (duō dà) is used to ask “how 
old”. Another example is 多高, which is used to ask “how tall or 
high”. For example, 你多大 (How old are you) and 你多高 (How 
tall are you).
