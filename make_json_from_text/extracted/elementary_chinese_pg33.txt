Grammar Notes: 
1.  Greeting with 你好 (nǐ hǎo, hello, literally “you good/well”): 
This expression is often used to greet people whom you meet 
for the first time. 您好 “nín hǎo” is the respectful form of “nǐ 
hǎo”—it’s used to express special respect for the addressee. It 
is typically used when meeting people more senior in age or 
status or when greeting customers. In such situations the reply 
is often simply 你好 or 您好.
