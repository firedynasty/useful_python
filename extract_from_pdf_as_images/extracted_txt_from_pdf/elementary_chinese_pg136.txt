Grammar Notes: 
1.  Descriptive complement: 
The particle 得 is often used after a verb or an adjective to 
describe the verb before it, as in the structure: Verb / Adj. + 得 得
+ Descriptive complement. For example, 我写得慢。In this 
sentence, 得慢 is the descriptive complement, used to describe 
the verb 写. It is similar to the English word “slowly”. Here are 
more examples: 
我的中文老师说得很快。(My Chinese teacher speaks Chinese 
fast.) 
她唱得很好听。(She sings well.) 
你学得很好。(You learned well.) 
2.  The adverb 真 (zhēn): 
It is used only in exclamatory sentences and comes before an 
adjective. For example, 你真好! (“you are truly nice”). In this 
sentence, 真 is used to modify 好. More examples: 
他真帅！ 
你们真漂亮！ 
美国真大啊！
