Mediterranean Feta Salad

Ingredients:
• 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
• 1 cucumber, chopped
• 1 bell pepper (red or yellow), chopped
• 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
• 1/2 cup Kalamata olives (pitted)
• 1/2 cup feta cheese, crumbled
• 2 tablespoons olive oil
• 1 tablespoon red wine vinegar (or lemon juice)
• 1 teaspoon dried oregano (or fresh if you have it)
• Salt and pepper to taste
• Fresh parsley or basil, chopped (for garnish, optional)

Instructions:
Prepare the Vegetables:
• In a large bowl, combine the halved cherry tomatoes, chopped cucumber, chopped bell pepper, sliced red onion, and Kalamata olives.
Add Feta:
• Crumble the feta cheese over the top of the salad mixture.
Make the Dressing:
• In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, red wine vinegar (or lemon juice), dried oregano, salt, and pepper.
Combine:
• Drizzle the dressing over the salad and gently toss to combine, ensuring that the feta cheese stays intact.
Garnish and Serve:**
• If desired, sprinkle fresh chopped parsley or basil on top for additional flavor and color.
• Serve immediately or let it sit for about 10-15 minutes to allow the flavors to meld.
