Grammar Notes: 
1.  The verb 觉得 (jué dé): 
This word has two meanings: “feel” and “think”. In the sentence 
“你觉得怎么样” from the dialogue, here 觉得 expresses 
a feeling, sensation, or perception. The word 觉得 can also 
indicate that you are expressing a falsifiable opinion or 
assertion. You have mulled over a set of facts and have settled 
on a conclusion. For example, 
我觉得她很漂亮。I think she is pretty. 
我觉得纽约很大、很美。I think New York is big and pretty. 
2.  The word 哪里:

in English. The structure is “Subj. + Verb + 哪里 ?哪里 ” 哪里, which
