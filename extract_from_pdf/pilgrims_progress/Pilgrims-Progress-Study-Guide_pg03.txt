Reflect and Discuss
1) Have you ever thought of the Christian life as a pilgrimage? Why might that be a helpful metaphor?
2) What does Christian discover about his condition in the opening scene (p. 19-20)? How does he make that 
     discovery? Why is that significant for us today?
3) What big decision does Christian make in response to this discovery (p. 22)? Is that the same as being 
     “saved”? How do you know?
4) What role does Evangelist play in this story? What might that teach us about our role in evangelism today?
5) Read Matthew 10:32-39. How does this passage inform our response to Christian leaving his family the way 
     that he does?
6) How does Christian’s encounter with Pliable and Obstinate help us understand some of the kinds of people 
     we will meet in our journey through the Christian life? 
7) How would you describe the Swamp of Despond? In what ways has that reflected your experience?
8) What difference did the Swamp of Despond make in the journeys of Christian and Pliable? Why was 
     Christian able to continue his journey after falling into the swamp?
