Chapter 6: The Appointment of Deacons; Stephen Accused
----------------------------------------
Henry shows how the church wisely organized to meet practical needs, freeing apostles for their primary work, while introducing Stephen whose faithful service led to martyrdom.

Key theological themes:
1. **Discord in Growth**: As disciples multiplied, "there arose a murmuring" - the Grecian (Hellenist) widows were allegedly neglected in daily distribution. Henry notes: "The first contention in the Christian church was about a money-matter." Even in the best-ordered church there will be grievances; "those are the best that have the least and the fewest."

2. **Apostolic Priorities**: The twelve said, "It is not reason that we should leave the word of God, and serve tables." Not that charitable work was unimportant, but their calling was higher. "Preaching the gospel is the best work, and the most proper and needful that a minister can be employed in."

3. **Qualifications for Deacons**: They must be "of honest report, full of the Holy Ghost and wisdom." Good reputation, spiritual maturity, and practical judgment are all required. The people nominated candidates; the apostles ordained them with prayer and laying on of hands.

4. **The Two Great Ordinances**: Henry identifies "word and prayer" as the minister's chief work: "By the word God speaks to them, and by prayer they speak to him." These maintain communion between God and his people.

5. **The Church's Increase**: When order was established, "the word of God increased" and disciples "multiplied greatly" - even "a great company of the priests were obedient to the faith." Good administration promotes gospel growth.

6. **Stephen's Character**: He was "full of faith and power" and "did great wonders and miracles among the people." Though appointed to serve tables, he excelled in spiritual gifts.

7. **Opposition to Stephen**: When opponents "were not able to resist the wisdom and the spirit by which he spake," they "suborned men" to bring false charges of blasphemy against Moses and God, against the temple and the law.

8. **Stephen's Countenance**: Before the council, "all that sat in the council, looking stedfastly on him, saw his face as it had been the face of an angel" - a sign of divine approval and inward peace.

Practical applications:
- Growing churches need organized structures for practical ministries
- Ministers must prioritize word and prayer
- Faithful service in lesser offices prepares for greater usefulness
- False accusation often follows faithful witness
