Chapter 10: Cornelius and the Gentiles Received
----------------------------------------
Henry presents this chapter as a pivotal turning point: "Lo, we turn to the Gentiles." The door of faith is opened to the uncircumcised, breaking down the wall of partition between Jew and Gentile.

Key theological themes:
1. **Cornelius's Character**: A Roman centurion, "a devout man, and one that feared God with all his house," who gave alms and prayed continually. Great men and good men rarely meet, but here they did. His prayers and alms "came up for a memorial before God."

2. **The Angel's Message**: Though an angel appeared to Cornelius, the gospel must be delivered by a human minister. "It was not to the highest of angels, but to those who were less than the least of all saints, that this grace was given, to preach among the Gentiles."

3. **Peter's Vision**: On the housetop at prayer, Peter saw a sheet with all manner of creatures and heard, "Rise, Peter; kill and eat." His refusal ("Not so, Lord") showed adherence to the law. But the response was decisive: "What God hath cleansed, that call not thou common."

4. **God Is No Respecter of Persons**: Peter's great declaration: "In every nation he that feareth him, and worketh righteousness, is accepted with him." Ceremonial characters were abolished so that moral ones might be regarded. Neither birth nor nationality determines acceptance.

5. **Christ Preached to Gentiles**: Peter summarized the gospel - Christ's anointing, ministry of doing good, death on a tree, resurrection, and commission to preach. "Through his name whosoever believeth in him shall receive remission of sins."

6. **The Spirit Falls on Gentiles**: While Peter still spoke, the Holy Ghost fell on all who heard. Jewish believers were "astonished" that the Spirit was poured on uncircumcised Gentiles. They spoke with tongues and magnified God.

7. **Baptism Follows the Spirit**: "Can any man forbid water, that these should not be baptized, which have received the Holy Ghost as well as we?" The sign must not be denied to those who have the thing signified.

Practical applications:
- Prayer and alms must go together
- God works through human ministers, not angels, to preach the gospel
- Ethnic and ceremonial distinctions are abolished in Christ
- Where God gives his Spirit, we must not withhold fellowship
