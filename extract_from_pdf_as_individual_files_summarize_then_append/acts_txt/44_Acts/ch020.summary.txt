Chapter 20: Eutychus Raised; Paul's Farewell to the Ephesian Elders
----------------------------------------
Henry presents Paul's tender care for the churches, the danger of sleeping under preaching, and the apostle's moving farewell - a model for all ministers in doctrine, life, and pastoral concern.

Key theological themes:
1. **The Lord's Day Assembly**: "Upon the first day of the week, when the disciples came together to break bread" - the Christian sabbath, celebrated for Christ's resurrection and the Spirit's outpouring. They met in an upper chamber, "a garret," yet came together faithfully.

2. **The Long Sermon**: Paul "continued his speech until midnight," being ready to depart. "Farewell sermons are usually in a particular manner affecting both to the preacher and to the hearers." There may be occasion for ministers to preach "not only in season, but out of season."

3. **Eutychus Falls**: A young man "fell into a deep sleep" and fell from the third loft, "taken up dead." Henry warns against sleeping under preaching: "We must look upon it as an evil thing, as a bad sign of our low esteem of the word of God." God's severe visitation shows "how jealous God is in the matters of his worship."

4. **Life Restored**: Paul "fell on him, and embracing him said, Trouble not yourselves; for his life is in him." Like Elijah and Elisha before him, he raised the dead. "They were not a little comforted" - the miracle confirmed the gospel.

5. **Paul's Example Reviewed**: To the Ephesian elders he appealed: "Ye know, from the first day that I came into Asia, after what manner I have been with you at all seasons, serving the Lord with all humility of mind, and with many tears."

6. **Nothing Kept Back**: "I kept back nothing that was profitable unto you, but have showed you, and have taught you publicly, and from house to house." He testified to Jews and Greeks "repentance toward God, and faith toward our Lord Jesus Christ."

7. **Bound in Spirit**: "I go bound in the spirit unto Jerusalem, not knowing the things that shall befall me there: Save that the Holy Ghost witnesseth in every city, saying that bonds and afflictions abide me." Yet "none of these things move me, neither count I my life dear unto myself."

8. **Feed the Flock**: "Take heed therefore unto yourselves, and to all the flock, over the which the Holy Ghost hath made you overseers, to feed the church of God, which he hath purchased with his own blood." Grievous wolves would come, and from their own selves would men arise "speaking perverse things."

9. **Commended to God**: "I commend you to God, and to the word of his grace, which is able to build you up, and to give you an inheritance among all them which are sanctified." Ministers can do no more for their people than commend them to God's grace.

Practical applications:
- The Lord's Day is for assembly, worship, and communion
- Sleeping under preaching dishonors God and harms the soul
- Ministers must preach the whole counsel of God
- Life should not be counted dear when the ministry is at stake
- Elders must watch for wolves from without and within
