Grammar Notes: 
1.  Question particle 吗 (ma): 
It is often added to the end of a sentence to form a simple yes/
no question. Compare: 
你是学生。 nǐ shì xuésheng. You are a student. 
你是学生吗? Nǐ shì xuésheng ma? Are you a student? 
2.  The adverb 不 (bù , no, not): 
As an adverb, it is normally put before a verb or an adjective to 
negate them. For example, 不姓李 or 不好。Note that 不 (bù) is 
pronounced bú in front of a syllable carrying a fourth tone. So 
不是 is pronounced bú shì, and 不姓 is pronounced as bú xìng . 
This phenomenon is called Tone Sandhi. This video 
summarizes how to pronounce 不 in different situations. 
3.  The verb 是 (shì): 
The verb is similar to “am/is/are” in English, linking two noun 
phrases, with the second one characterizing or identifying the 
first. For example: 
我是学生。 Wǒ shì xuésheng. I am a student.
