Learning Objectives: 
After learning this lesson, you will be able to: 
•  know the four tones, neutral tones, and tone markers. 
•  pronounce consonants/initials and vowels/finals. 
•  understand the writing system, including basic strokes, 
character composition, and stroke order. 
• 
learn some basic expressions that are often used in class.
