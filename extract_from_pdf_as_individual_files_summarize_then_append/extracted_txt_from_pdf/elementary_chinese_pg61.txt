Grammar Notes: 
1.  The question word 几 (jǐ): 
It is used to ask how many? Note that it is used when asking 
about small numbers, usually less than 10. In addition, this 
word is usually followed by a measure word. Example: 
几口人? Jǐ kǒu rén? How many people?（Note: in most cases, 个 
is used before 人 to describe how many of people. 口 is only 
used to describe family members.） 
2.  两 (liǎng) vs 二 (èr)： 
The digit “2” is 二 , used generally in numbers, when counting 
to ten, telling a phone number, and so on. 两 is used to say 
there are “two” of something, generally followed by measure 
words. For example, “two girls” is translated as “两个女孩子” 
rather than “二个女孩子”. 
3.  The question phrase 哪个: 
It is used to express “which”. For example, “which is your elder 
sister” is translated into “哪个(人)是你的姐姐” in Chinese.
