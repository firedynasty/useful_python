Write a natural Chinese conversation between two people
about ordering food at a carinderia.

Format: A: is always the Chinese line, P: is the pinyin, B: is always the English translation
Each A line is immediately followed by its P pinyin, then its B translation.

[Scene 1]
A: 姐姐，今天有什么菜？
P: Jiějie, jīntiān yǒu shénme cài?
B: Ate, what are the dishes today?

A: 我们有adobo、sinigang和pinakbet。
P: Wǒmen yǒu adobo, sinigang hé pinakbet.
B: We have adobo, sinigang, and pinakbet.

[Scene 2]
A: 请给我一份adobo。
P: Qǐng gěi wǒ yī fèn adobo.
B: I'd like one adobo please.

Rules:
- A: is ALWAYS Chinese characters, P: is ALWAYS the pinyin of the A line, B: is ALWAYS the English translation
- Natural spoken Mandarin Chinese
- Group into [Scene 1], [Scene 2], etc.
- 15-20 exchanges total
- Keep lines short and conversational
- Pinyin must use tone marks (e.g. māmá, not mama)
