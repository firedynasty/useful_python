# 来自不同地方的朋友 / Friends from Different Places

---

[Scene 1 — First Meeting]

A: 你好！你是新来的吗？
P: Nǐ hǎo! Nǐ shì xīn lái de ma?
B: Hi! Are you new here?

A: 对，我刚到。你呢？
P: Duì, wǒ gāng dào. Nǐ ne?
B: Yes, I just arrived. And you?

A: 我来这里两年了。你从哪里来的？
P: Wǒ lái zhèlǐ liǎng nián le. Nǐ cóng nǎlǐ lái de?
B: I've been here two years. Where are you from?

A: 我是四川人。你呢？
P: Wǒ shì Sìchuān rén. Nǐ ne?
B: I'm from Sichuan. What about you?

---

[Scene 2 — Getting to Know Each Other]

A: 我是上海人。四川啊，你一定很能吃辣！
P: Wǒ shì Shànghǎi rén. Sìchuān a, nǐ yīdìng hěn néng chī là!
B: I'm from Shanghai. Sichuan! You must love spicy food!

A: 哈哈，对！不辣不香嘛。你们上海人喜欢甜的吧？
P: Hāhā, duì! Bù là bù xiāng ma. Nǐmen Shànghǎi rén xǐhuān tián de ba?
B: Haha, yes! It's not good without spice. Shanghai people like sweet food, right?

A: 没错，我们口味比较淡。你叫什么名字？
P: Méicuò, wǒmen kǒuwèi bǐjiào dàn. Nǐ jiào shénme míngzì?
B: That's right, our taste is milder. What's your name?

A: 我叫王磊。你呢？
P: Wǒ jiào Wáng Léi. Nǐ ne?
B: My name is Wang Lei. And you?

A: 我叫陈静。很高兴认识你，王磊。
P: Wǒ jiào Chén Jìng. Hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ, Wáng Léi.
B: My name is Chen Jing. Nice to meet you, Wang Lei.

---

[Scene 3 — Talking About Home]

A: 你在上海长大的吗？
P: Nǐ zài Shànghǎi zhǎng dà de ma?
B: Did you grow up in Shanghai?

A: 是的，从小就在那里。四川哪个城市？
P: Shì de, cóng xiǎo jiù zài nàlǐ. Sìchuān nǎge chéngshì?
B: Yes, I've been there since I was little. Which city in Sichuan?

A: 成都。你去过吗？
P: Chéngdū. Nǐ qù guò ma?
B: Chengdu. Have you been there?

A: 去过一次！大熊猫基地太可爱了。
P: Qù guò yī cì! Dà xióngmāo jīdì tài kě'ài le.
B: Once! The giant panda base was absolutely adorable.

A: 对吧！下次去我带你吃正宗的火锅。
P: Duì ba! Xià cì qù wǒ dài nǐ chī zhèngzōng de huǒguō.
B: Right?! Next time you go, I'll take you for authentic hot pot.

---

[Scene 4 — Wrapping Up]

A: 太好了！那我带你去外滩看夜景。
P: Tài hǎo le! Nà wǒ dài nǐ qù Wàitān kàn yèjǐng.
B: Wonderful! Then I'll take you to the Bund to see the night view.

A: 一言为定！我们加一下微信吧。
P: Yī yán wéi dìng! Wǒmen jiā yīxià Wēixìn ba.
B: It's a deal! Let's add each other on WeChat.

A: 好的，扫我的码。真高兴今天遇到你！
P: Hǎo de, sǎo wǒ de mǎ. Zhēn gāoxìng jīntiān yùdào nǐ!
B: Sure, scan my code. I'm really glad we met today!
