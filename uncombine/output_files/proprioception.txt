How Can Proprioception Be Improved?
Proprioceptive abilities can be enhanced through training. Exercises often involve balance challenges, such as:

Standing on one leg (with eyes open or closed).

Balancing on uneven or unstable surfaces (like foam pads or balance boards).

Performing sport-specific drills that require quick changes in direction and dynamic balance.

These exercises force your body to rely on proprioceptive feedback rather than vision, strengthening the neural pathways that control joint stability and muscle coordination.
