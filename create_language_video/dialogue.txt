[Scene 1]
A: Uy, natanood mo 'yung FilipinoP od101 video?
B: Hey, did you watch that FilipinoP od101 video?

A: 'Yung about sa pag-introduce ng sarili? Oo, kanina ko pa napanood.
B: The one about introducing yourself? Yeah, I watched it earlier.

A: Ang dali pala, 'di ba? Tatlong minuto lang.
B: It's actually easy, right? Just three minutes.

A: Oo nga. Sabi nga nila — fastest, easiest, and most fun.
B: Right. Like they said — fastest, easiest, and most fun.

[Scene 2]
A: So 'yung una, kamusta lang pala 'yung "hi" sa Filipino?
B: So the first one, kamusta is basically just "hi" in Filipino?

A: Oo, pero literal na ibig sabihin — "how are you."
B: Yes, but the literal meaning is "how are you."

A: Parang "hola" sa Español?
B: Kind of like "hola" in Spanish?

A: Medyo. Galing din kasi sa Spanish — "como está."
B: Sort of. It actually came from Spanish — "como está."

[Scene 3]
A: Tapos susunod, "ako si" — tapos pangalan mo na?
B: Then next is "ako si" — then just your name?

A: Tama. So pag ikaw — "Ako si..." — tapos sabihin mo name mo.
B: Correct. So for you — "Ako si..." — then say your name.

A: Ang simple lang pala!
B: That's so simple!

A: 'Di ba? Tatlong parts lang — kamusta, ako si, at 'yung "nice to meet you."
B: Right? Just three parts — kamusta, ako si, and the "nice to meet you."

[Scene 4]
A: Pero 'yung formal version — paano ulit 'yon?
B: But the formal version — how was that again?

A: Dagdag mo lang ng "po." Kamusta po — ganon.
B: Just add "po." Kamusta po — like that.

A: Ah, 'yung "po" at "opo!" Natutunan ko 'yan dati pero nakalimutan na.
B: Ah, the "po" and "opo!" I learned that before but I forgot already.

A: Lahat ng Pilipino natututo n'yan pagbata pa lang kasi.
B: All Filipinos learn those when they're still very young.

[Scene 5]
A: Para sa matatanda, 'di ba?
B: For elders, right?

A: Oo, kapag nakikipag-usap ka sa mas nakatatanda — po lagi.
B: Yes, whenever you're talking to someone older — always use po.

A: Tapos 'yung full name — mas pormal na agad.
B: And using your full name — that's automatically more formal.

A: Oo nga! Sa Pilipinas, first name muna bago last name.
B: Exactly! In the Philippines, first name always comes before the last name.

A: Sige, subukan ko — "Kamusta po? Ako si [name]. Ikinagagalak kong makilala kayo!"
B: Okay let me try — "Kamusta po? Ako si [name]. It is a pleasure to meet you!"

A: Haha! Galing! Pilipino ka na agad!
B: Haha! Great job! You are basically Filipino now!
