Write a natural Filipino/Tagalog conversation between two people
about ordering food at a carinderia.

Format: A: is always the Filipino line, B: is always the English translation.
Each A line is immediately followed by its B translation.

[Scene 1]
A: Ate, ano po ang ulam ngayon?

A: Meron tayong adobo, sinigang, at pinakbet.
B: We have adobo, sinigang, and pinakbet.

[Scene 2]
A: Pabili po ng isang adobo.
B: I'd like one adobo please.

Rules:
- A: is ALWAYS Filipino, B: is ALWAYS the English translation of the line above
- Natural spoken Filipino, include contractions ('di, 'yung, 'ko, pa'no)
- Group into [Scene 1], [Scene 2], etc.
- 15-20 exchanges total
- Keep lines short and conversational
