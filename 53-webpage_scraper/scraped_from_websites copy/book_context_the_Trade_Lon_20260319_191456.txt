# The Trade: Moving From the Life You Have to the Life You Want

**By Lon Stroschein**

## Book Overview

The Trade offers a proven framework to elevate your life and unlock your true potential. This compelling playbook provides insights and practical guidance to help professionals embrace transformative change, specifically targeting those at the top of their careers who feel frustrated, isolated, and questioning their true purpose.

In 2022, Lon defied conventional wisdom by leaving his prestigious C-suite job on a hunch that life demanded more from him. Without a resume, business plan, or vast wealth, he shed the big office, title, and image, embarking on a journey to unlock his life's potential. When he resigned, his inbox overflowed with messages from high-achievers worldwide seeking the same path but lacking guidance.

## Core Concept: Normal 40

Lon has identified a phenomenon he calls "Normal 40" - the growing tension between who you have become and who you are capable of being next. This feeling represents the internal struggle between staying safe and taking a leap of faith to explore uncharted territories. He has since built a movement dedicated to giving a home to those quietly wrestling with career or military transitions.

The book serves as a playbook for working through your own Normal 40 Moment, helping readers understand they are not alone and that this gut feeling, when understood properly, is actually a gift.

## Key Themes and Insights

**The Second-Half Story**
The Trade challenges readers to live their second-half story without regrets. It dares professionals to break free from their status quo, remove self-imposed limitations, and embark on a journey of self-discovery. The book emphasizes that every life culminates in a final day surrounded by loved ones, prompting reflection on legacy and life choices.

**The Ultimate Trade-Off**
The central premise revolves around weighing risks and embracing two prevailing thoughts: "I am glad I did it" or "I wish I would have tried." The book inspires readers to seize every moment and make choices that align with their truest self, ensuring they end up glad they took action.

**Beyond Financial Independence**
Unlike typical FIRE (Financial Independence/Retire Early) books, The Trade addresses finances briefly and in a non-FIRE way. This approach makes it accessible to those not on the FIRE path who are looking for ways to take meaningful leaps anyway.

## Research and Methodology

Lon's pursuit led him to conduct thousands of one-on-one interviews with individuals navigating their most critical life transitions. These conversations and the tough decisions we all encounter fueled his drive to write this book. The research revealed that the feeling of professional unfulfillment among successful executives is widespread but rarely acknowledged.

## Practical Application

**For Unfulfilled Executives**
The book specifically addresses those who find reasons to keep going at work: hoping the next level/job will restore passion, chasing titles and curated images, dealing with pride and shame about walking away from decade-long investments, and struggling with legacy concepts.

**Community and Support**
Beyond writing a book, Lon has created an online community serving as a support group for those inclined to make their Trade. The movement includes plans for in-person events and regional groups offering local support and encouragement.

## Target Audience and Impact

The Trade is designed for professionals experiencing specific feelings:
- "What am I doing? Is this all there is? Am I settling because it's easy?"
- Those wondering "Shouldn't I feel better about what I'm doing with my life?"
- People feeling guilty about considering changes that might affect family promises
- Anyone with outward trappings of success but feeling lost, adrift, and purposeless internally

## Author's Mission

Lon's mission is to inspire change in a thousand lives. He will measure success by receiving a thousand notes of thanks from people whom the change has impacted. The book represents not just personal transformation advice but the foundation of a growing movement.

## Reader Testimonials and Outcomes

**Immediate Impact**
Readers report finishing the book in 24-48 hours, finding it shocking how closely the content mirrors their own feelings and thoughts. Many describe it as the most introspective yet easiest to grasp book about oneself.

**Practical Results**
- Provides tools for maximizing the next life chapter
- Helps readers understand they're not alone in wanting career fulfillment
- Equips individuals with knowledge for making informed decisions about change
- Offers ways to articulate previously hard-to-express feelings

**Emotional Resonance**
The book includes deeply personal family struggles that add authenticity and emotional depth. Readers report chapters that bring them to tears, particularly when Lon opens up about personal challenges.

## Writing Style and Approach

The Trade is described as:
- Free from corporate jargon and academic concepts
- Written with humility and real-world applicability
- "Curious, not critical" in its approach
- Offering comfort (that you're not alone) and empowerment (that change is within reach)
- Delivering "gut punches" - challenging revelations readers realize they needed

## Key Message

This book is fundamentally about getting yourself back - figuring out what you like and don't like, what you're going to tolerate and what you're not. It emphasizes progress without the need for perfection that holds people back. The Trade is not about quitting your job; it's about reclaiming your authentic self and making intentional decisions about your life's direction.

The time for action is now, and readers make their own decisions about what trades they want for their second half of life.
