# ✍️ Today’s assignment
Yesterday you identified a few things you explained to someone this week. Today, you’re going to take one of those things as a topic to write more about. Here’s the prompt:
> Pick one explanation from yesterday’s list — whichever one feels most interesting to you, or maybe the one where you thought, “I wish I’d known this earlier.”
> Open a new draft in Medium and write for 15 minutes about what you wish someone had told you before you learned that thing. Not a polished article, just stream-of-consciousness writing. Start with something like:
> “I wish someone had told me that…”
> “Before I learned [topic/thing/skill], I thought…”
> “The thing nobody tells you about [topic/thing/skill] is…”
> Don’t worry about structure or making it perfect. Just get your thoughts on paper.
> The point: You’re transforming your everyday expertise into writing. [“What I Wish I Knew” is a powerful, simple format](/blog/the-highly-specific-yet-oddly-relatable-wisdom-of-what-i-wish-i-knew-8102241af99c) that helps you recognize where you have expertise.
> Tomorrow: You’ll look at what you wrote (without judging it).
> Congrats, you’ve written something today.
