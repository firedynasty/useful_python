Come write with me!
We all of us have little notions of stories we’d like to write, or ideas we’d like to share. Maybe it’s even fuzzier for you, just an impulse somewhere in you to _want to write_. Creating the right conditions for that impulse to turn into something more — believing we have something worth sharing, giving that thing shape, getting it out into the world — now that’s the challenge.
A few months back, I was in a weekly meeting with 
[Brittany Jezouit](https://medium.com/u/9b63206eb11c?source=post_page---user_mention--d8a50f0e384a---------------------------------------)
(Medium’s chief of staff) and the topic of writing came up. I mean, the topic of writing comes up in just about every meeting—we are a reading and writing company after all!—but this was about our own writing. We both said we wanted to be writing more, and being the action-oriented person she is, Brittany suggested we become accountability partners. The idea was just to nudge each other along to write, a bit more. Nothing crazy. Just get some more stories out into the world.
Now it’s nearly summer. I’ve [written some for work since then](https://blog.medium.com/your-story-matters-join-us-on-april-25th-for-draft-day-2025-923a150133f4), but it’s not the kind of writing Brittany’s been holding me accountable for. (Brittany, of course, has been better about actually publishing than I have—[read up on where to eat ice cream in Richmond, VA](/rooted-publication/an-ice-cream-tour-of-richmond-va-20c3fa610109)).
I have ideas for things I want to finish. But day in, day out, the rest of life takes over. Finding the time is hard. And the blinking cursor on my screen is daunting. Does any of this sound familiar? I bet it does.
So as June starts, I set myself a simple goal: Publish one story on Medium by the end of June.
And rather than doing this all by my lonesome, I want to do it with you. And with other folks from Medium!
So join us for a little experiment this month, the June “Write With Medium” challenge. We’re launching a daily (Monday-Friday) newsletter, which will offer micro challenges each day, little assignments and prompts that will help you build a habit over time and give you a framework for writing your story. These will be easy, asking less than 15 minutes of commitment each day.
Sign up here:[https://medium.com/not-perfect/newsletter](/not-perfect/newsletter)
The first email goes out June 3rd, and then every weekday through June 30th. Along the way, we’ll have some time to write together virtually, and find accountability partners for those interested. By the end of June, you’ll have a story ready to publish on Medium.
There’s so much that’s hard about writing; a big part of it is how solitary it can feel, both when you’re doing it, and afterwards if you have something you want to publish. Will anyone read it, and will anyone care.
But we’re building Medium to help solve a lot of these problems, to make writing and sharing that writing with the world (or with just one person) as simple as possible. So join us, let’s write our stories together.
