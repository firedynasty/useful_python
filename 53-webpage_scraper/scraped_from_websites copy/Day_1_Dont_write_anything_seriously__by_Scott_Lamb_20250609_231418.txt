# ⚡️ Today’s assignment
This one couldn’t be easier, though it may challenge you in unexpected ways. I tried it out yesterday (I’m road-testing all of this with you) and it was — well, I don’t want to color your experience. Here’s the prompt:
> Open a new draft in the Medium editor (on a web browser, just type in “story.new” in the address bar; in the app, hit the little green pen/paper icon in the lower right).
> Set a timer for 5 minutes.
> DO NOT WRITE ANYTHING.
> Instead, pay attention to what comes up for you as you’re staring at the blank page. This exercise is just about seeing what starts to arise naturally for you when you have some space, quiet, and nothing else distracting you. After the timer goes off, you can make a note about anything you want to remember for the future.
