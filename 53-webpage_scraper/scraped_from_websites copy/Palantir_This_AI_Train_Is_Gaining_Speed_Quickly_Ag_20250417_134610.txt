## Palantir: AI Rocket Ship Demonstrates Resilience
I must say I'm enthralled by the incredible recovery of Palantir Technologies Inc. (NASDAQ:[PLTR](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/PLTR#hasComeFromMpArticle=true#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "Palantir Technologies Inc.")), outperforming not just the market, but also its software peers. As a result, what seemed like a massive drawdown From the highs about two months ago met with highly resilient dip buying momentum, despite trading at what seems like an astronomical earnings multiple that some pessimists might contend as unsustainable.
In my [previous article on PLTR,](https://seekingalpha.com/article/4767760-palantir-finally-time-to-buy-before-the-market-rebounds-upgrade#hasComeFromMpArticle=true#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link) I had already noted that the stock fell into a bear market after notching stupendous gains to begin 2025. While President Trump’s Liberation Day tariffs did cause market mayhem that led to a broad decline across tech and growth stocks, PLTR’s bottom last week coupled with this week’s early recovery appears to have retaken much of its March losses, browbeating against the bearish prognosticators, although [short-sellers appear few](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/pltr) and far between with a short interest ratio of just under 2.5% as of end March 2025.
In other words, I believe PLTR’s market outperformance suggests that the market has confidence that:
  * Palantir seems more insulated against the tariffs-induced shock that has inundated its semiconductor and hardware peers. And since Palantir explicitly doesn’t sell its programs and services to China, we shouldn’t expect significant repercussions from the tit-for-tat trade conflict between Donald Trump and Xi Jinping. Yet, we must also not misconceive that there could be blowback from the trade war with global ramifications on enterprise spending, as it remains a crucial growth vertical for Palantir while seeking to diversify its reliance on DoD contracts to justify its expensive growth valuations.
  * The market also seems assured that the [earnings profile for application software vendors](https://yardeni.com/charts/application-software/) like Palantir is generally not as badly impacted as the general market. Earnings estimates for the application software industry have remained resilient in April, as compared to the [negative revisions for the S&P 500](https://yardeni.com/charts/sp-500-nrri-neri/) ([SPX](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/SPX#hasComeFromMpArticle=true#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "S&P 500 Index"), [SP500](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/SP500#hasComeFromMpArticle=true#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link)). Hence, it corroborates my opinion that software stocks should continue to outperform even if the trade war were to worsen. The sustained outperformance of PLTR and its software peers ([IGV](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/IGV#hasComeFromMpArticle=true#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "iShares Expanded Tech-Software Sector ETF")) over the semiconductor industry ([SMH](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/SMH#hasComeFromMpArticle=true#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "VanEck Semiconductor ETF")) since June 2024 underpins my belief that the market has renewed its focus on the second-order derivative, as AI software platforms or providers become increasingly valuable in the AI supply chain, as they capitalize on their network effect to drive growth and operating leverage, as seen with the surge in valuations and [active user base for OpenAI](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/chatgpt-revenue-surges-30-just-three-months?rc=3e6wgn) recently.
  * [Palantir’s recent NATO contract win](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4430897-palantirs-nato-deal-places-it-in-prime-position-for-ai-in-defense-wedbush#hasComeFromMpArticle=true#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link) marks pivotal progress and validation of the strength of its underlying tech as the transatlantic steps up its defense spending. In addition, it could also open up more opportunities for Palantir in the EU, as Europe seeks to adopt best-in-class AI tech, although trade tensions may escalate, tempering the region’s enthusiasm to work with American tech partners in the near term.

And if that’s not all, consider that [Palantir is the best-in-class](https://blog.palantir.com/palantir-joins-forces-with-u-s-army-for-project-convergence-capstone-19c79f3c748a) AI platform for the defense department, and is expected to feature even more prominently in America’s bid to make sure China doesn’t catch up with its AI leadership, while modernizing the defense infrastructure with [budget reallocation toward AI imperatives. ](https://blog.palantir.com/palantirs-recommendations-to-the-white-house-ostp-on-developing-an-ai-action-plan-80d2f1821e8b)
President Trump’s recent executive order to [revive America’s shipbuilding industry](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-04-11/anduril-saronic-saildrone-aim-to-bring-drones-to-the-ocean) has already driven a surge in collaborations with AI defense providers, suggesting that the opportunities to benefit from the [massive defense budget](https://www.politico.com/news/2025/04/07/hegseth-trump-1-trillion-defense-budget-00007147) (which could reach a record $1T) cannot be downplayed. Hence, I believe it indicates that even if we were to meet cyclical headwinds due to a slowdown in enterprise spending in 2026, it might not be detrimental to Palantir's growth cadence if the defense component could pick up the slack.
## Palantir: Defense Business Provides Robust Support Amid Turbulence
[](https://static.seekingalpha.com/uploads/2025/4/16/saupload_188cf62d4da9209fa1b46233ccda3fc8.jpeg)
Palantir revenue segments (FinChat)
The silver lining is that government revenue accounts for almost 55% of its revenue base over the past twelve months. While commercial revenue has gained traction as enterprise companies reacted positively to its modular go-to-market approach through its AIP boot camps, it seems like Palantir’s government business remains its bread and butter, notwithstanding its more lumpy contractual volatility.
Given a more dangerous geopolitical climate and the U.S. government’s focus on raising defense spending and also restructuring the allocation to AI, I believe PLTR’s ability to benefit must be emphasized. As I enunciated in my previous article, the superiority, and advantages emanating from its AI platform can be entrenched, given how effective its ontology has proved to its defense partners.
Hence, even if there were to be a deceleration in enterprise spending, I believe it might amount to a pause, but not cancellation, suggesting any delays shouldn’t be construed as perpetual. And, it’s critical to point out that Wall Street remains highly [confident in Palantir’s earnings](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/PLTR/earnings/revisions#hasComeFromMpArticle=true#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link) resilience. Hence, the company could be well-poised to continue dominating and gain share against the legacy contractors as they struggle with the restructuring while fending off [challenges from other AI companies](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4430235-openai-ceo-sam-altman-leaves-door-open-to-pentagon-but-is-cautious#hasComeFromMpArticle=true#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link) keen on carving out their market share amid the AI transformation and modernization journey.
## Buy Or Sell PLTR Stock?
[](https://static.seekingalpha.com/uploads/2025/4/16/saupload_f8bb39dd900613cc7ac0986f861b30a3.png)
PLTR price chart (weekly, medium-term) (TradingView)
With that in mind, I believe it shouldn’t have surprised you that PLTR’s bottom in April just above the $65 level attracted dip buyers with conviction about the company’s ability to weather the macroeconomic headwinds while relying on its core defense spending prowess and potential resilient enterprise base as we get through the recessionary calls by macro forecasters and strategists.
PLTR’s price action strongly implies that unless the April lows are rejected, the ongoing recovery isn’t expected to falter, notwithstanding near-term volatility in the broad market. Given [PLTR's relatively high beta](https://www.barrons.com/articles/palantir-stock-price-pentagon-77a69882?mod=md_stockoverview_news), investors are encouraged to add progressively on pullbacks and not chase FOMO surges or price spikes to improve their risk/reward profile.
Nevertheless, I must also highlight that with a forward EBITDA multiple of almost 140x, it isn’t for the faint of heart. If a recession were to occur, it could torpedo its valuations multiples as compared to much less expensive peers with a software median of just over 15x. In other words, the weight is on Palantir to substantiate its growth thesis, while providing clarity that its enterprise and government spending can be maintained, notwithstanding the consequences of the trade restructuring between America and its trading partners.
Yet, a sustained bottom also provides the driving force for buying momentum to improve further, as investors reallocate to solid outperformers like PLTR, paving the way to revisit its 2025 highs and augmenting a plausible breakout thesis subsequently.
Rating: Maintain Buy.
