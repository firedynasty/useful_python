Ron Friedman's "The Best Place to Work: The Art and Science of Creating an Extraordinary Workplace" (2014) explores how to build productive, engaging work cultures using psychology, behavioral economics, and neuroscience. The highly-rated business book (4.15-4.18 stars on Goodreads) provides science-backed strategies for improving office environments.

Key themes include fostering curiosity, embracing "play" at work, and cultivating positive social connections. The book offers actionable advice such as promoting autonomy, fostering workplace friendships, and embracing failure to boost motivation and creativity.

Counterintuitive findings demonstrate how small changes can dramatically impact workplace performance. Examples include adding a fish bowl to improve focus and using techniques from hostage negotiators to resolve workplace conflicts. The award-winning psychologist and founder of Ignite80 presents these insights in an engaging, conversational style.

The book is praised for containing "surprising" insights and practical applications. It's widely recommended for leaders, managers, and anyone seeking to improve their professional environment. Reviews highlight its ability to combine latest scientific research with actionable strategies for creating extraordinary workplaces.
