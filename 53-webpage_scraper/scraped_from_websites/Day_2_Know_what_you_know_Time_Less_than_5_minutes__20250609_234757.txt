# 💡 Today’s assignment
So here in Day 2, it’s about starting to pay attention to your expertise. Most people think they have nothing interesting to write about. They’re wrong — they just don’t know how to identify their own knowledge. Here’s the prompt:
> I want you to notice 2–3 things you explained to someone this week. Not “what’s the wifi password,” but more substantial questions. Where were you able to jump into “explainer mode” without even thinking twice about it? For each one, jot down:
> — What they asked
> — Why they asked you (not Google or ChatGPT)
> — What knowledge you have that let you answer their question
> The point: You already know things others find valuable. These moments reveal your expertise. Writing isn’t always about having extraordinary experiences. It’s about recognizing the value in your ordinary expertise. The goal here is to start noticing where (and about what) you already have a story to tell.
