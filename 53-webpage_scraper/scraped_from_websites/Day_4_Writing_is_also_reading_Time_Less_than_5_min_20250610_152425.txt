Yesterday, you wrote. Something, maybe not perfect, but words on the screen. It may have been hard, but I hope it felt good. This is progress.
# 📝 Today’s assignment
I want to end the week on a light note, to keep up the momentum and also save some energy for the weeks ahead. Today’s assignment should only take a few minutes, but I hope holds a useful lesson about learning not to judge, but to see what’s actually there. Today’s prompt:
> Read what you wrote yesterday— don’t edit anything, just read
> Take note of anything that surprised you : An insight you didn’t realize you had. A new connection you made. A different way of putting something.
> The point: First drafts aren’t supposed to be polished , they’re supposed to help you see what you’re thinking. Often the most interesting parts are the ones that surprise you as you’re writing them. You’re learning to see potential instead of problems.
Next week we build structure. Today, just notice what’s already working.
