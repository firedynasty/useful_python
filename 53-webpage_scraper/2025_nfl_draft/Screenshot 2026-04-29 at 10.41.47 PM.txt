             
  ---                                                                           
  scrape_prospect_grades.py Ñ Interactive tool that builds a combined 2025 NFL  
  Draft dataset by merging data from three sources:                             
                                                                                
  Data Sources:                                                                 
  - Pro Football Reference Ñ Full draft pick list (rounds 1-3): player name,    
  team, position, age, college                                                  
  - NFL Combine xlsx (~/Downloads/NFL Combine 2025 @JordanSportGuy Twitter.xlsx)
   Ñ Physical measurements: height, weight, hand size, arm length, wingspan,    
  40-yard dash, 10-yard split, vertical, broad jump, 3-cone, shuttle, bench     
  press                                                                       
  - NFL.com prospect pages Ñ Prospect grade (scraped interactively)             
                  
  How It Works:                                                                 
  1. Automatically scrapes the draft table from Pro Football Reference (rounds
  1-3, ~100 players)                                                            
  2. Loads combine measurements from the xlsx file and matches players by name
  3. Walks you through each player interactively Ñ shows the name, you Google it
   in Chrome, navigate to their NFL.com prospect page, press Enter, and the     
  script reads the grade from the open page                                     
  4. Merges everything into a single CSV                                        
                                                                                
  Controls during interactive scraping:                                       
  - Enter Ñ read grade from current Chrome page                                 
  - s Ñ skip player                            
  - q Ñ save progress and quit                                                  
                              
  Resume support: Saves progress to grade_progress.csv after each player, so you
   can quit and pick up where you left off.                                     
                                                                                
  Output: 2025_nfl_draft_with_combine.csv with 20 columns (draft info + prospect
   grade + combine measurements). Height and body measurements are converted to
  readable format (e.g. 6'1 5/8", 31 4/8").                                     
                  
  Requires: Chrome running with --remote-debugging-port=9222
